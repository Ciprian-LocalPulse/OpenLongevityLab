# Support OpenLongevity

OpenLongevity is open scientific infrastructure: provenance-preserving evidence records, transparent scoring, and reproducible research tooling for the biology of aging. It is built in the open and stays free to use, fork, and audit.

Maintaining it costs real time and money — data-adapter upkeep, server and database costs, CI/CD, security review, and the hours spent keeping the evidence pipeline correct and trustworthy. If OpenLongevity is useful to you, in your research, your product, or your own learning, please consider supporting its development.

**What your support funds:**
- Keeping the PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, and ClinicalTrials.gov adapters running and up to date
- Infrastructure: PostgreSQL hosting, CI pipelines, staging environments
- Time spent on evidence-scoring accuracy, contradiction surfacing, and reproducibility
- Documentation, onboarding, and community review

Financial support is entirely optional. It does not purchase scientific conclusions, priority review, or access to private research data — see [`RESEARCH_ETHICS.md`](RESEARCH_ETHICS.md) and [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) for how the project keeps funding and findings separate.

Every contribution, of any size, genuinely helps and is appreciated.

## Other Ways to Help

Money isn't the only way to support the project — and for an open scientific tool, these matter just as much:

- **Star and share the repo** — visibility helps attract reviewers and contributors.
- **Contribute code, tests, or documentation** — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Review evidence records or report data-quality issues** — scientific scrutiny is core to the project's integrity.
- **Cite OpenLongevity** in your own work — see [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).

Thank you for considering supporting OpenLongevity. 🧬
