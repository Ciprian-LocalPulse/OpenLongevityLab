# The OpenLongevity Academic Manifesto

OpenLongevity is guided by evidence before hype, reproducibility before publicity, transparent uncertainty, and collaboration. Human evidence remains distinct from animal and cellular evidence; correlation remains distinct from causation; and every result carries provenance and limitations.

OpenLongevity does not exist to promise immortality. It exists to make aging research more transparent, connected, reproducible, and computationally accessible.

The project uses responsible AI: machine extraction is labeled, source links are retained, and an AI output cannot become an evidence record without review. Privacy, licensing, data integrity, and scientific correction are first-class concerns.
