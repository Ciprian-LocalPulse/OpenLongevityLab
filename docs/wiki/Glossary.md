# Glossary

- **Evidence level** — A transparent label for study-design maturity.
- **Healthspan** — Time lived in good functional health; distinct from lifespan.
- **Replication** — Independent confirmation of a reported finding.
- **Provenance** — Source and transformation history of a record.
- **Biomarker** — A measured feature associated with a process; not automatically causal or definitive.
